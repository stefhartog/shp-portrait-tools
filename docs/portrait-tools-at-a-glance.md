# SHP Portrait Tools

**Portrait retouching with a shared sense of scale, selection and control.**

## Skin Tools

The foundation is the combination of **subject scale S** and an **automatic, adjustable Skin Mask**: scale-aware controls adapt to the subject, while the mask guides where corrections belong.
Frequency separation, skin-tone correction and dodge & burn share that foundation, so you can work with each individually, move between them, or bring them together in one combined result.

Use Skin Tools as a **PhotoCraft companion**: send the processed layers with **Send Layers**, then refine masks and layer opacity in PhotoCraft to strengthen or soften the effect exactly where you want it.
Automation gets you started; professional, hands-on control is yours whenever you want it.

### Skin Mask

Start with automatic skin detection, then refine coverage with feathering, light and color ranges, and structure protection.
Inspect the analysis and adjust scale landmarks when needed: automation gives you a starting point, not the last word.

### Frequency Seperation & Tone

Separate broad color and shading from fine detail, with scale-calibrated radii, optional skin-aware Blur and pose-directed Directional Blur.
Bring skin hues closer together with adjustable Skintone Correction, while retaining control over the detail layer and the correction's strength.

### Dodge & Burn

Balance local brightness differences with two independently adjustable feature-size bands, applied through the shared mask.
Tune the scale of the work and its strength rather than treating every tonal variation alike.

### Combine

Bring your configured FS & Tone and Dodge & Burn treatments together with independent **FS mix** and **D&B mix** controls.
For a series, local batch processing analyzes each image's scale and mask while applying your chosen settings.

## AI Fill

Two complementary tabs share one source and ComfyUI connection, with separate generation settings and presets.

### Crop & Expand

Reframe the portrait and generate the extra surroundings with FLUX.2 or FLUX.1 Fill.
Compare variations, choose your composition, and send the expanded canvas and generated layer back to PhotoCraft.

### Patch Fill

Paint what you want to replace, generate alternatives with FLUX.1 Fill, and choose how much surrounding context the model sees.
Only the painted area is composited back; PhotoCraft receives a masked patch layer without a canvas resize.

## Settings & Info

Keep appearance, output format, layer-mask placement and performance preferences in one place, separate from your retouching presets.

**Keep the finish in your hands.** Skin Tools can send a flattened result or editable ingredients to PhotoCraft; locally loaded images save numbered results beside the source.
